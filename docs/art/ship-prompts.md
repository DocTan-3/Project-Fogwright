# The last ship: prompts

The trader's ship from Hollin's Cross that carries the survivors away (prologue beat F): a small two-masted brig with
light steampunk touches (one of the few ships with a small steam engine; Hollin's Cross is richer and nearer Aldrath
than Farhallow). Make the concept image first, then feed the chosen image to Meshy's Image to 3D.

## Concept image

> Game asset concept, three-quarter view on a plain light grey background: a small two-masted merchant brig, about 16 m
> long, with light steampunk touches. A dark tarred clinker-and-carvel wooden hull with a pale painted stripe and a
> carved trailboard at the bow. A raised quarterdeck at the stern with a small deckhouse and a ship's wheel. Two masts
> with furled off-white canvas sails on yards, and a bowsprit with a jib. Amidships: a compact brass-and-iron boiler
> with a single tall riveted black funnel, banded with brass, a thin trail of smoke, and a small brass steam whistle. A
> sternwheel or a modest screw propeller housing at the stern, iron-strapped. Cargo on deck: barrels, crates under a
> canvas tarp, coiled rope. A boarding gangway on one side, lanterns at the bow and stern. Weathered but cared-for,
> working ship. Realistic proportions, clean low-poly game style, flat-shaded, muted earthy palette (dark wood, brass
> ochre, iron grey, off-white canvas), no fantasy glow, no characters, no water.

## Meshy (text or image to 3D)

> Low-poly game-ready model of a small two-masted merchant brig with light steampunk details, 16 metres long: dark
> wooden hull with a pale stripe, raised quarterdeck with a small deckhouse and ship's wheel, two masts with furled canvas
> sails on yards, bowsprit with a jib, a compact brass-and-iron boiler amidships with one tall riveted black funnel
> banded in brass, an iron-strapped sternwheel at the stern, barrels, crates and coiled rope on deck, a boarding gangway
> on one side, lanterns at bow and stern. Clean simple shapes, realistic proportions, readable silhouette.

**Negative:** no water, no ocean base, no characters, no flags with text, no fantasy glow, no ornate gold filigree, no
cannons, no oversized gears, no paddle wheels on the sides, no floating parts, no base plate.

**Settings:** triangle topology, about 15,000 target polycount (cut to 8-10k in Blender), symmetry on, bow forward with
the waterline level, simple or flat-colour textures (the palette replaces them).
