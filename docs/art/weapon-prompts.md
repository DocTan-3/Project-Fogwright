# Weapon prompts

Prompts for image generators (2D concept sheets) and text- or image-to-3D generators (models). Make the 2D sheet first,
check it, then feed the chosen picture to an image-to-3D tool with the 3D prompt; that keeps a set consistent.

## The style block (paste into every prompt)

> Fogwright style: realistic proportions, clean low-poly game asset, flat-shaded with simple hand-painted colour, no
> photo textures, no fantasy glow unless stated, muted earthy palette (iron grey, ash wood, worn leather brown, brass
> ochre), light steampunk only where stated, readable silhouette from a distance.

## 2D concept sheet (one per set)

> Game weapon concept sheet, orthographic side views on a plain light grey background, all items to the same scale with
> a 1 m ruler, labelled, evenly spaced: **[ITEM LIST]**. One matching set: **[SET DESCRIPTION]**. [STYLE BLOCK]. No
> characters, no hands, no text other than the labels.

## 3D model (one per item)

> Low-poly game-ready 3D model of a **[ITEM]**, **[SET DESCRIPTION]**, **[ITEM DETAILS]**. Real-world scale **[LENGTH]**,
> **[TRIANGLES]** triangles or fewer, single material with one texture, clean quads where possible, no internal faces,
> pivot at the main grip, pointing straight up. [STYLE BLOCK]
