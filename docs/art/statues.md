# Statues of the gods

The twelve stand in Aldrath's grand plaza, facing the sealed walls (lore.md). Below are **proposed looks** for each, to
approve or change; the 2D and 3D prompts are written once the looks are chosen. Each statue: about 4 m tall on a plinth
carved with the god's sign, weathered pale stone, in the same pose language (a strong, readable silhouette from the
plaza's far side).

| God | People | Proposed look | Held / pose | Sign on the plinth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Aldrath the First** | Head of the Gods | A tall robed lawgiver with a plain circlet; serene face, but the hood throws a shadow over the eyes (a quiet hint that he is the 14th) | a sealed book against the chest, a rod of judgment held upright | a closed gate with a keyhole |
| **Hrothvar the Unbowed** | Hrimfolk | Broad, bearded, in furs and riveted mail, leaning into a gale | one foot on a wave-carved rock, both hands on a great axe planted like an anchor | an anchor crossed with an axe |
| **Maelis the Steward** | Aurelans | A regal woman in a long cloak and simple crown | a sheaf of wheat in one arm, a builder's square in the other hand | a tower rising from wheat |
| **Seraveth the Remembering** | Ilvari | Slender and veiled, looking back over the shoulder | a small reliquary lantern held up, a scroll unrolling to the ground | an open eye within a book |
| **Ostrak the Anvil** | Ferrin | Short and immensely strong, braided beard, leather apron | a hammer raised over an anvil, mid-strike | an anvil |
| **Khesra the Flowing** | Zarhim | Wrapped in robes caught in motion, as if by a desert wind | pouring water from a jar onto the sand at her feet | a droplet inside a crescent |
| **Brannoch the Bondkeeper** | Ruadhan | A cloaked chieftain, hair in braids | one hand on a great hound at his side, the other holding a braided cord | an endless knot |
| **Hollin the Wayfarer** | Wayfolk | A traveller in a wide-brimmed hat and long coat, a pack on the back | a walking staff with a hanging lantern, the other hand open in welcome | a waystone with its lamp |
| **Oriel the Lucid** | the Animari (lost) | An androgynous figure whose one side turns into clockwork and gears | holding a prism up to the eye | an eye inside a gear |
| **Vael the Unseen** | his people (lost) | Hooded, the face entirely hidden | stepping through a stone arch, a key in hand | an arch |
| **Verdane the Open-Handed** | taught all | Barefoot, hair of leaves and vines | both palms open: a seedling in one, a small bird in the other | an open hand with a leaf |
| **Galeth the Vigilant** | the Lantern Vigil | An armoured knight | a tower shield planted in front, a lantern raised high | a lantern over a shield |

**Merrith the Merciful** (the 13th, gone): *(proposal)* no statue in the plaza; an empty thirteenth plinth that no one
speaks of. Her only images are small and humble, like Farhallow's shrine: a kneeling woman with cupped hands holding a
light.

**To decide:** each god's look (keep, change or replace); whether gods are shown as men, women or neither as proposed;
whether the plaza has the empty thirteenth plinth.
